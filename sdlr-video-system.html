The Process VSLTrust · ~6 min
[On camera, calm and conversational, in a finished backyard]

If you are planning to remodel most or all of your backyard, one of the biggest mistakes you can make is asking for a construction price before the entire space has been properly planned.

A beautiful backyard is not just a collection of nice features. The pool, hardscape, patio cover, outdoor kitchen, landscaping, lighting, drainage, and utilities all need to work together as one cohesive space.

That is why we created the San Diego Landscape Remodeling Whole Yard Planning Process.

Over the next few minutes, I will show you how it works and how it can help you avoid expensive mistakes before construction begins.

Even if you never hire us, this will help you ask better questions and compare your options more confidently.

[B roll: a dramatic before and after of the same property. The finished space in use for outdoor cooking, poolside living, family gatherings, relaxing, and entertaining]

Our goal is not simply to install pavers, build a patio cover, or add a few attractive features.

It is to create an outdoor space that feels like a natural extension of your home. A place designed around how your family actually wants to live.

Not just a yard you look at through the window, but a space where you can cook, entertain, relax, and enjoy living in San Diego.

[On camera]

Here is where many backyard projects go wrong.

They begin as disconnected ideas.

A pool company handles the pool. Someone else installs the pavers. A third contractor builds the patio cover.

Then the details connecting everything, including drainage, elevations, utilities, lighting, access, and material transitions, are considered too late.

[B roll: drainage conditions, elevation changes, utilities, narrow access, unfinished transitions]

The problem is not necessarily the people performing the work. The problem is the fragmented plan.

When every company focuses only on its own piece, responsibility becomes fragmented and important details fall between scopes.

That is when homeowners encounter mismatched materials, unexpected change orders, drainage problems, construction delays, or finished work that must be removed and completed again.

[On screen: a short excerpt from a real client review about planning or communication] [On camera]

Before accepting a price from anyone, ask one important question:

Is everyone pricing the same clearly defined finished project?

If the answer is no, the lowest estimate may only be the lowest starting point.

[B roll: SDLR owners walking a property, taking measurements, reviewing inspiration photos]

That is why we begin by looking at your entire property.

We learn how you want to use the space, establish your priorities, evaluate the existing conditions, and discuss the investment involved.

Then we use our Whole Yard Planning Process to determine the right path from your first idea through the completed project.

Phase One  |  Discover
[B roll: project form, initial phone call, property walkthrough, measurements, grade, drainage, access, sunlight]

First, you complete our project form and tell us about your property and what you want to create.

Once we receive it, we call you to learn more about your goals, scope, timing, and investment expectations. We answer your initial questions and make sure San Diego Landscape Remodeling is the right fit for your project.

If we agree that it makes sense to continue, we schedule an in person property walkthrough.

During that walkthrough, we evaluate the entire space, including the grade, drainage, access, utilities, sunlight, surrounding structures, and how your family wants to use the yard.

If the vision, property conditions, and budget need to be adjusted, it is better to discover that now than after you have spent money in the wrong direction.

Phase Two  |  Plan
[B roll: designer walking the property, plans, measurements, renderings, material selections, completed projects]

Next, we determine how much planning and design the project requires.

A clearly defined project can move toward preliminary pricing. A complex project involving several connected features, structural work, elevation changes, or major layout decisions should begin with professional design.

When design is the right path, we recommend the designer we believe is best suited to your project and explain why. When appropriate, we can introduce more than one option based on the project's style, complexity, and design needs.

Design is not simply about creating a beautiful picture.

It allows the important decisions to be made before construction begins and gives the homeowner, designer, contractor, and trades one coordinated plan to follow.

You are not guessing, and neither are we.

[Insert a short real client clip about feeling confident before construction]
Phase Three  |  Price and Prepare
[B roll: detailed proposal, material selections, plans, permit documents, scheduling, team coordination]

Once the project is clearly defined, we prepare a detailed construction proposal tied to the agreed scope.

You should understand what is included, what is excluded, which decisions have been made, and what could affect the final investment.

The goal is not simply to hand you a number. It is to help you understand exactly what that number represents.

Before construction begins, we coordinate the selections, materials, permits, scheduling, and major phases of the build.

Decisions made on paper are easier and less expensive to change than decisions made after construction has already started.

Phase Four  |  Build and Stand Behind It
[B roll: property protection, demolition, underground work, drainage, construction details, owners on site, cleanup, completed spaces]

During construction, you have a clear point of contact and regular communication about what is happening, what is coming next, and which decisions need your attention.

The two owners of San Diego Landscape Remodeling remain directly involved in the projects we take on.

You are not handed from a salesperson to people you have never met after signing an agreement. The people who help establish the expectations remain involved in delivering on them.

We also pay attention to the details you may never see once the project is finished. The preparation, drainage, underground utilities, elevations, and structural connections are just as important as the visible finishes.

When construction is complete, we conduct a final walkthrough with you.

At approximately ten months, we return to walk the property again. This gives the space time to experience regular use, irrigation, weather, and seasonal changes while giving us another opportunity to confirm that everything is performing as intended.

We do not want to disappear when construction ends. We want you to know who stands behind the work.

[On screen: another real review about communication, craftsmanship, or how SDLR handled the entire project] [On camera]

If you are ready to explore what your entire outdoor space could become, click the button below and request your backyard consultation.

Tell us about your project, and our team will call you to learn more, answer your initial questions, and determine whether we should schedule an in person property walkthrough.

There is no pressure to sign a construction contract. The first step is simply gaining clarity and discovering the right path forward.

Your family's future favorite place might be waiting just outside your back door.

The Three Fears, coldTrust · ~2 min · 03A

For homeowners who have not met you yet. Why us page, lead nurture, retargeting. CTA: request a consultation.

[On camera, direct to lens]

If you are considering a major backyard remodel, you probably have a few concerns about choosing the right contractor, even if you have not said them out loud.

Most homeowners are worried about the same three things.

Let's talk honestly about each one and show you how our process is designed to address them.

Fear One  |  Will they disappear?
[B roll: owners meeting homeowners, active construction, client communication, a final walkthrough]

The first fear is, "They will start, take my money, and disappear."

San Diego Landscape Remodeling is a licensed contractor, and you can verify our license before trusting us with your property.

But a license alone does not guarantee communication or accountability.

That is why both owners remain directly involved from the first property visit through the final walkthrough. You have a clear point of contact, regular communication, and people you know standing behind the work.

We even return at approximately ten months to walk the completed project with you again.

Fear Two  |  Will the price keep climbing?
[B roll: detailed proposal, written scope, allowances, material selections, change documentation]

The second fear is, "The price will keep climbing once construction begins."

No honest contractor can promise that a project will never change. A homeowner may add to the scope, and hidden property conditions can sometimes be discovered during construction.

What we can do is define the scope clearly, identify allowances, explain what is included, and document changes before additional work moves forward.

You should always understand why a price changed and approve that decision before paying for it.

Fear Three  |  Will it match the vision?
[B roll: inspiration photos, design plans, renderings, material selections, plans to completed projects]

The third fear is, "What if the finished yard looks nothing like I pictured?"

That is why complex projects begin with a clear plan.

When professional design is needed, we connect you with a designer we trust so the layout, materials, dimensions, and major details can be worked out before construction begins.

You can see and approve the direction before we start building it.

[On camera, direct and reassuring]

A good contractor should not tell you that nothing can ever go wrong.

A good contractor should show you the process they use to prevent problems, communicate changes, and remain accountable when decisions need to be made.

If these concerns have been keeping you from starting, click Request Your Backyard Consultation below.

Tell us about your project, and our team will call you to answer your initial questions and discuss the right path forward for your space.
