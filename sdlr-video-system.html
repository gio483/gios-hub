Help the homeowner avoid the expensive mistake

A homeowner spending fifty to three hundred thousand dollars on their backyard is not buying landscaping. They are buying certainty, and a person they believe will not disappear. The most powerful thing you can do on camera is not sell. It is teach them how to avoid the mistake most people make, then show them a calmer way. Below is the full system: the Whole Yard Planning Process VSL and seven supporting scripts, each built to move one feeling at one moment in the funnel, grounded in Alex Hormozi's value and lead frameworks and the real psychology of a high end remodel buyer.

The Hormozi levers every script is pulling

The Value Equation

Every video either raises the top of the equation or lowers the bottom. Raise the dream and the belief it will actually happen. Lower the time, the effort, and the risk.

Value = (Dream x Belief) / (Time x Effort and Risk)

The give, then the consult

Give away the most valuable thing you can, free. The concept image of their own backyard is the give. Once trust is earned, the only ask is a consultation, not a contract.

Risk reversal

The buyer's fear is a big irreversible mistake. One honest number, founders present, a concept before they commit, and an honest recommendation even if it means design comes first, all remove that fear.

Attract and filter

Hormozi's lead work is about attracting and filtering profitable leads, not chasing the highest volume. So the videos qualify for the homeowner planning a full transformation and quietly wave off the small repair shopper.

The Whole Yard Planning Process, your canonical language

The VSL is built around a named, four phase process. Use these exact four names everywhere a buyer looks, the website, this VSL, the post visit video, and the estimate, so your process reads the same at every touchpoint. Consistency across touchpoints is its own trust signal.

Phase One

Discover

Project form, a call, and an in person walkthrough of the whole property.

Phase Two

Plan

Decide how much design the project needs, and recommend the right designer when it does.

Phase Three

Price and Prepare

One detailed proposal tied to a defined scope, then coordinate everything before the site is disturbed.

Phase Four

Build and Stand Behind It

Owners involved, clear communication, a final walkthrough, and a return at ten months.

What is actually going on in the buyer's head

They do not want to be sold. They want to be helped. At this price point, a homeowner is quietly afraid of making a costly mistake they cannot undo. The company that teaches them how to avoid it, calmly and without a pitch, becomes the one they trust to run it. Education is the sale.

Contractor fear is the dominant emotion. Almost everyone has a story: a crew that vanished, a number that doubled, strangers in the yard they never met. Their real question is not "are you talented," it is "will you finish, will you tell me the truth about money, and who will actually be here." Answer those three and you have most of the sale.

The yard is identity and status. It is where they host, relax, and show who they are. So sell the vacation, not the flight. Show the family using the space, not the paver spec.

Certainty reads as expensive and safe. Calm, specific, and honest beats loud and clever every time at this price point. The tone of these scripts is the message.

The Process VSL

The flagship. Not a commercial. A founder helping a homeowner avoid an expensive mistake.

Stage  Trust Length  About 6 min Lives  Homepage, landing page, YouTube, sent before a consultation Talent  Gio or Mike, educational tone, project footage throughout CTA  Request Your Backyard Consultation

On screen title: Before You Remodel Your Backyard, How the SDLR Process Works

Why it works

This is the full value equation in one sitting, delivered as help rather than a pitch. It qualifies the right homeowner in the first ten seconds, raises the dream, then names the real problem, fragmented planning, without attacking anyone. The four phase walkthrough lowers the fear of the unknown, and proof is woven throughout instead of stacked at the end, so belief builds the whole way down.

The close asks only for a consultation and openly promises clarity, even if that means design before pricing. Promising the honest path instead of a contract is the strongest trust signal in the whole film. Give the "is everyone pricing the same clearly defined finished project" line a real beat on the day, it is the money moment.

A beautiful backyard is not just a collection of nice features. The pool, hardscape, patio cover, outdoor kitchen, landscaping, lighting, drainage, and utilities all need to work together as one cohesive space.

That is why we created the San Diego Landscape Remodeling Whole Yard Planning Process.

Over the next few minutes, I will show you how it works and how it can help you avoid expensive mistakes before construction begins.

Even if you never hire us, this will help you ask better questions and compare your options more confidently.

Where projects go wrong
[On camera]

Here is where many backyard projects go wrong.

A pool company handles the pool. Someone else installs the pavers. A third contractor builds the patio cover.

When every company focuses only on its own piece, responsibility becomes fragmented and important details fall between scopes.

[On screen: a short excerpt from a real client review about planning, problem solving, or communication] [On camera]

Before accepting a price from anyone, ask one important question:

The three fears

One trust core, two placements. Same three fears, a different frame and CTA depending on whether they have met you yet.

Why it works

Naming a fear the buyer never said out loud makes you the trusted authority. The honesty on price, that no contractor can promise a project will never change, is more believable than a frozen number, and the close, that a good contractor shows you the process rather than promising nothing goes wrong, is the differentiator.

Production

How to shoot the whole set in one morning

All of your on camera pieces are you, in the same look. Shoot them together so your light, wardrobe, and energy stay consistent, then edit into the individual videos. Here is the order and the notes that matter.

Priority order, by leverage

If you only get through a few, do them in this order. Each earns its keep the moment it goes live.

  1. The Process VSL. The one asset that does the most work. It is longer and needs the most b roll, so give it the real time. Put it on the homepage, behind the consultation page, and on YouTube.
  2. Free concept, 60 and 15. This feeds the top of the funnel and the mailer you already built. It is the give that starts everything.
  3. Three fears and Founders on every project. The two trust videos that turn a curious visitor into a believer.
  4. After we leave your house. Wire it into the post visit follow up so it runs on autopilot.
  5. One honest number and Who this is not for. The closers that protect your margin and your time.
