The play — read this first (2 min)

KSH is execution-only and its #1 lead source is designers & architects. So the strategy is two-track, not one:

Track 1 · Own the referral engine (designers, architects, the pros)

These rooms are cheap, recurring, and full of the exact people who hand KSH $1M+ projects. The move isn't to "attend" — it's to become a known regular. Join ASID San Diego as an Industry Partner and NKBA San Diego, then show up monthly. Add ProVisors for the wider circle of realtors, wealth managers and attorneys who all touch the same coastal homeowner. This is Gio's lane, year-round.

Track 2 · Stand next to the homeowner (car culture + the social circuit)

Coronado / Point Loma / La Jolla / Rancho Santa Fe wealth gathers at concours, polo Sundays, and marquee galas. You don't sell here — you get known and liked, and you meet them where the designers already are too. This is where Kyle is the face for the high-net-worth rooms; Gio works the recurring, lower-cost ones.

Highest-leverage moves

1) Rancho Santa Fe Cars & Coffee — free, every Saturday, in the wealthiest zip in the county. 2) La Jolla Concours VIP tier (April) — pay to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with collectors for hours. 3) Sponsor a home tour (La Jolla Modernism, Oct) — your brand in front of design-obsessed homeowners. 4) Host your own designer + agent mixer at The Lodge at Torrey Pines — puts KSH at the center of the room, not the edge.

Bring-to-Kyle note: memberships (ASID IP, NKBA, BIA, a car club) are the real budget ask — a few hundred to ~$1–2K each, but they convert one-off attendance into standing access. Frame them as designer-referral pipeline, not "events."
