SDLR Logo Animations
Three intro / outro stings
v1 · Nov 9, 2026

Three different animated versions of the SDLR logo, built from the real logo vectors — not a picture of the logo being faded in. Every one comes as an intro (3 seconds, for the front of a video) and an outro (4.2 seconds, with the website, phone and licence number), in both landscape and vertical. Pick the one that fits the video.

How to use these

The MP4s are ready to drop straight onto a timeline — they already have the brand background behind them. Use these for anything posted as-is.

The ALPHA files (.mov, ProRes 4444) have a real transparent background, so the logo sits on top of your footage with nothing behind it. Use these when you want the mark to animate over a backyard shot. They are drawn in cream, not gold — that is the brand guide's "over photography" colour, and it stays readable over both bright sun and shade, where gold washes out. They are too large for this page, so they live in the client folder — path at the bottom. A gold set can be made any time.

Every one ends on a held final frame, so you can freeze or extend the last frame as long as you like, and fade out on your own terms. There is no sound on any of them.
