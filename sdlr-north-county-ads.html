Decision rules

  • Don't judge a hook before about 2,000 impressions.
  • Don't judge an offer until each version has spent about $300 or produced 5 leads.
  • A winner needs a clearly lower cost per A lead, about 30% or more.
  • Pause any ad with 5+ leads and zero A leads, however cheap.
  • Change one thing at a time.

At $50k to $100k tickets lead volume is low. Early rounds read hook rate, CTR and A lead share. Cost per signed design needs 6 to 8 weeks.

Strategy

Escondido, Poway, Vista, San Marcos, Rancho Bernardo, 4S Ranch. Whole yard remodels, front or back, roughly $50,000 to $100,000.

The argument every ad makes

  1. Problem: a big yard project feels like a gamble. Will it look like we discussed? Will the price balloon? Will I be chasing people?
  2. Why it goes wrong: most projects start from a rough idea and a rough number, so every decision nobody made up front becomes a change order or a shortcut.
  3. Fix: design the whole yard first, price that exact design, and run it with one coordinator.
  4. Offer: design starts at $4,500 and is credited back in full on qualifying projects you build with us.
  5. Trust: licensed, one honest number, a dedicated project coordinator, the ten month walkthrough, real Google reviews.

Four pillars

  • Organized project management. One coordinator, a real plan, no chasing anyone.
  • Low stress. You're not the one managing the job.
  • The yard you actually discussed. Not a cheap knockoff of what you were shown.
  • The price you were quoted. No surprise change orders at the end.
