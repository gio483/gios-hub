04 Selections library — preferred brands

Defaults the estimator agent suggests. You can override anywhere.

Pavers / hardscape
Belgard Dimensions, Mega-Arbel, Catalina Stone · Techo-Bloc Blu, Aberdeen, Industria · Eldorado Stone for veneer. Polymeric joints in graphite or stone for restraint.
Pool coping & waterline
Travertine bullnose · honed limestone · large-format porcelain. Waterline tile from Mike's specialty material network.
Pergolas
Cedar with satin walnut stain (warm) · powder-coated aluminum or steel (modern). Slatted, louvered, or solid waterproof roof. Integrated 2700 K down-lighting in beams.
Outdoor kitchens
Danver stainless cabinetry (matte black or champagne) · honed black granite or Dekton counters · Lynx or Wolf grills · stainless or fluted-stone backsplash.
Decking
Trex Transcend · Fiberon Concordia · IPE for the hero spend. Hidden clip fasteners. Color: warm gray or weathered teak default.
Lighting
FX Luminaire · Coastal Source · Kichler · Hinkley. 2700 K everywhere — always. Smart controller at panel.
Turf
SYNLawn Cool Plus · EasyTurf. SBR-free infill (cooling). Hidden bender-board edge. Pile 1.5"–2".
Plants — anchor palette
Mediterranean default: olive (Olea Wilsonii) · Italian cypress · Westringia Wynyabbie Gem · Lomandra Breeze · agave/aloe accents. Modern desert and coastal as alternates.
Fire features
Round 48" gas pit clad in honed limestone (warm) or board-form concrete (modern). Fireplaces faced in Eldorado or stacked stone. Always natural gas, never propane unless required.
Drainage
NDS channel drains at patio edges · French drain at slope · catch basins at low points · pop-up emitters to daylight. Spec separately, never bury in hardscape.
Material pricing tier shorthand (internal) E = entry · M = mid · L = luxury · Hero = top-of-line. Log every selection's tier in 04-selections.md so the investment range stays reconciled when a client upgrades a single item.
05 Render prompts — Freepik

Two prompt templates. Reimagine when a drone shot exists (image-to-image, preserves the actual yard geometry — best for hero). Mystic for concept hero shots from scratch.

Quick decision

Drone or aerial photo of the property → Reimagine (image-to-image, 0.55–0.7 strength).
No usable photo, or hero "moment" from scratch → Mystic (text-to-image, photorealistic preset).

06 Brand rules quick reference

The estimator agent already enforces these. This is the cheat sheet for when you're writing or editing manually.

Banned in client-facing prose

dream backyard transform your space your vision, our expertise starting at $20K from $20K minimum project size our studio visit our showroom same-day immediate best budget most affordable cheapest price match maintenance plan tree trimming

Use instead

by design, not by dispatch built slowly, built once full backyard remodels small-scope work (filter) our office in La Mesa (NAP only) for the backyard you actually use one honest number References available on request

Naming rules

  • NeverName our Field Lead by full name. Use "our Field Lead" or "our lead craftsman."
  • NeverName Mike's other companies in public copy. If material depth needs context: "a separate specialty company Mike also operates."
  • AlwaysReference Mike's CSLB license #1139785 on the proposal cover.
  • AlwaysUse city / neighborhood on the proposal — never the full address.

AI render rules

  • AlwaysCaption every render exactly: "Concept visualization — not a photograph of completed work."
  • NeverPass an AI render off as portfolio or completed work.
  • FlagIf a Freepik render contains text, brand logos, or rendered signs — re-run.

Pricing language

  • NeverPut the $20K floor in any client-facing prose. Internal only.
  • AllowedPer-service investment bands in a services table (e.g., "Retaining walls $20K – $100K") — context for serious readers, not a filter.
  • AllowedScope-language filters: "small-scope work," "design-build at this scale," "full backyard remodels."

Engagement model (mention in every proposal)

  • IncludeThe 10-Month Walk-Through (free post-completion inspection).
  • IncludeDirect-Founder Access (Gio + Mike on every project, no account managers).
  • IncludeSingle-crew, scheduled cadence — "by design, not by dispatch."
