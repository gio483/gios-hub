Services you can read from the doorstep

Gio changed some wording, pointed out that GotPrint's slit runs off to the side, and wasn't sold on the small services line on the front. Version 6 carries his wording, sets the services in two lines of white capitals as wide as the headline, and draws the slit the way GotPrint cuts it.

What changed

  • Front services: PERGOLAS · PATIOS / FIRE PITS · OUTDOOR KITCHENS in white capitals, gold dots between, set as wide as the headline. "Designed and built by one firm." now sits on its own line under them.
  • "Fire features" became "fire pits": the plainer word, and the one people search for.
  • Reason I: Gio's line, with the grammar fixed: "Ensuring it goes smoothly and stays on budget."
  • Die slit: drawn at an angle from the hole to the top edge, as on GotPrint's own hangers. It is a guide only: GotPrint's die makes the cut, and nothing but sky or photo sits in its path.
  • Spacing: tightened so the headline still clears the fire table.
