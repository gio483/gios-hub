Questions

The honest answers

Is this insurance?

No — and we're careful never to call it that. Standby is a vetted backup network. You're paying for access to a ready bench of pros and a guarantee that someone steps in, not a cash payout. That keeps it simple and clean.

Who pays the backup photographer?

You do — directly — and only if you actually activate one. It's about 80% of your normal shoot fee, agreed up front in a contract between you and the backup. Standby never touches that money, which protects everyone.

How do you know the backups are any good?

Every backup is hand-screened before they join the bench: 5+ years shooting weddings, a reviewed portfolio, references, and a reliability score that drops if they ever miss a check-in. Good people first, great shooters second.

What are you actually guaranteeing?

That a qualified, vetted professional is available and ready to step in. We don't guarantee a specific style or a certain number of photos — that's between you and your backup. We're the safety net, not the artist.

What if I never use it?

Most people never will — that's the whole point. You pay a small fee for peace of mind, the backups earn a little for being ready, and the wedding goes off without a hitch. Like a spare tire you're glad you never needed.
