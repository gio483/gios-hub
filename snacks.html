Keep the salsa, change the scooper

Salsa and hot sauce are already great: about 10 to 20 calories for 2 tablespoons. The chips are where the calories hide. A handful of regular tortilla chips is about 140 to 150 calories, and it's never one handful.

Creamy dips, rebuilt

Greek yogurt ranch1 cup plain Greek yogurt + 1 to 2 tbsp Hidden Valley ranch seasoning + a splash of milk. About a third of the calories of bottled ranch, with protein. Keeps 5 days in the fridge.
Greek yogurt French onion1 cup plain Greek yogurt + half a packet of onion soup mix. Let it sit 30 minutes so the onion softens. Tastes like the sour cream version.
Creamy salsaHalf salsa, half plain Greek yogurt, plus hot sauce to taste. Great with cucumbers and carrots.
Buffalo dipPlain Greek yogurt + Frank's or Cholula + garlic powder. For when you want something spicy.

Get the big tub of plain Greek yogurt for dips (Kirkland at Costco is the cheapest). Plain tastes tangy on its own, but once seasoning goes in you won't notice.

Fresh food, zero waste

The rule: fresh stuff only lasts a few days, so buy just what these 4 days use. Everything else on the board comes from the pantry, fridge and freezer, which you can stock up on.

Fresh buy list: 1 pack strawberries, 1 pack blueberries, 1 bag snap peas, 1 tray mini cucumbers, 1 bunch grapes, 1 small fresh salsa. (Baby carrots and whole jicama last 2 weeks or more, so they aren't on the clock.)

Same day you shop: wash the grapes, pull them off the stems and freeze half. Make one batch of Greek yogurt ranch.

Day 1 berries first

  • Strawberries + string cheese
  • Cucumber rounds + fresh salsa
  • Night: frozen grapes

Day 2

  • Two Good yogurt + blueberries
  • Snap peas + Greek yogurt ranch
  • Meat stick when you're on the road

Day 3 finish strawberries

  • Last of the strawberries (or freeze them)
  • Jicama sticks with lime + creamy salsa
  • Night: Yasso bar or Jello cup

Day 4 clear it out

  • Finish snap peas and cucumbers
  • Leftover blueberries go in the freezer
  • Fresh salsa should be gone. Switch to a jar.

Days 5 and beyond: run on pantry and freezer (jerky, meat sticks, SkinnyPop, frozen berries, edamame, Yasso, string cheese, yogurt) until your next shop, then repeat.
