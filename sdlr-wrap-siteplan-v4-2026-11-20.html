A Rancho Santa Fe backyard, planted deep

Just the backyard this time, about 200 by 150 feet behind a hacienda, with planting doing as much work as the stone. An upper terrace holds the outdoor kitchen, pergola dining, the covered loggia and a fireplace lounge. Below sit the pool and spa on a travertine deck, the lawn with a jacaranda island, a pickleball court behind a cypress screen, a fire terrace and a bench nook. Every other foot is beds: drifts of groundcover, shrub masses, ornamental grasses, agaves, boulders, olives, citrus, a coast live oak and a sycamore.
