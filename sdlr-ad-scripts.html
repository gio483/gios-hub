#1Founder-led, results-in-advance93/100
Hook 12 → Hesitation + Social Proof + UVP → Offer 2 → CTA 6

Tightest single promise in the set — the whole ad says one thing: a founder will design your actual backyard before you risk a dollar. Hook 12 is results-in-advance (the strongest play for a firm with no track record), Offer 2 pays it off literally, and CTA 6 closes the founder-access loop the hook opened. Believability is carried exactly where a brand-new firm is weakest — identification, the render as a real result, and the 10-Month Walk-Through. Nothing to fix for a first project; this is the cleanest cold-start ad in the package.

#2Damaging-admission trust play88/100
Hook 4 → Hesitation + Social Proof + UVP → Offer 2 → CTA 3

The honest "we're not the fastest, and if you need a crew this weekend we're the wrong call" is one of the strongest cold openings for a category people distrust — and it doubles as qualification, repelling the tomorrow crowd and echoing "by design, not by dispatch." It sits just behind #1 because the hook earns trust but doesn't itself promise the render the way Hook 12 does. Swap CTA 3 for CTA 6 if you want the founder thread carried to the end.

#3Recognition / emotional-want85/100
Hook 2 → Pain Point + Service + Social Proof → Offer 1 → CTA 4

Highest pure recognition — the right La Jolla homeowner instantly pictures their own backyard and their family. A textbook cold opening (emotional, not offer-led), with CTA 4 carrying the family thread all the way through. It trails #1 and #2 on believability: it leans on desire more than proof, and a firm with no testimonials yet needs the proof lever pulled harder — so it's the best top-of-funnel awareness ad rather than the hardest lead-driver. Raise it by moving Social Proof earlier, or leaning on the render's results-in-advance pull.

▸ Strategy & the offer gate (read once, not spoken)
Audience & geo. San Diego County luxury homeowners; hooks rotate real high-end pockets and build qualification (full-remodel scale) into the hook — never a price floor.
Offer — Gate 0: PASS. A private in-home design consultation with a founder that produces a photorealistic design of the client's actual backyard before they commit — a real result in advance. Believability (the hard part at launch, with no testimonials yet) is carried by founder identification, a reframed 25+ year track record and GC depth, the design-first / one-contract process, the render of their own yard, and the 10-Month Walk-Through standing in as risk reversal. It escapes a price fight through process and accountability, not a discount.
Voice. Editorial-luxury with human warmth — the client voice governs, not a generic contractor register.
Compliance held. No testimonials or specific delivered-job claims (none exist yet); "references on request"; no client-home visits; delivery = "our own crew and vetted specialty subcontractors, under one licensed contract"; no naming of the partner's other companies or the lead craftsman's full name; no price floor in prose; no emergency/same-day language.
Not the Seven Day Number. Built on the standard design-consultation offer, so that campaign's three open decisions stay open.

Open items

  • Which founder on camera — written so either Gio or Mike can deliver it; pick who's more natural for cold audiences.
  • Proof upgrades fast — once the first project is 30 days post-handover with a signed client quote, upgrade the Social Proof block from reframed track record to a real, attributed result.
  • The Seven Day Number's three open decisions (guarantee, price anchor, blocked CTA 3) remain untouched.
  • Product-named version available — Belgard, Techo-Bloc, Warming Trends, StruXure, SYNLawn, etc. can be worked into the Service / Social Proof blocks for a warmer audience.
