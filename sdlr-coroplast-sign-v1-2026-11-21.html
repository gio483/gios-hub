San Diego Landscape Remodeling · Coroplast yard sign · Version 1

A cheap, two-sided sibling of the metal sign

A 24 × 18 in coroplast sign that costs a few dollars, staked on a wire H-stake. Gio wanted it double-sided with different art on each side.

Flat, both sides

Side 1 on the left, side 2 on the right. They print back to back on one board.

Mockups: each side on a wire H-stake on a San Diego corner.

What changed

  • Round 1: four directions. A road check (what 20/30 eyes see at 60 and 100 ft) showed BACKYARD REMODELS reads furthest.
  • Round 2: every message in cream and green, offered as pairs. Gio picked BACKYARD REMODELS + "Ready to remodel your backyard?" and a small QR on the green side.
