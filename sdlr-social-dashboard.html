The three acts of the 90 days

Days 1–30

Foundation & Proof

Look established from post one. Lead with your strongest existing assets (Mike's LA project, the Field Lead's past builds), build out Highlights, and warm the local audience with undeniable transformations. Goal: a feed that signals luxury and momentum, plus your first 300–500 real local followers.

Days 31–60

Momentum & Authority

Now they trust the eye — show them the brain. Materials education, process craft, the founders, neighborhood design character, and the tech edge. Goal: saves, shares, and DM conversations. People start tagging their spouse.

Days 61–90

Offer & Referral

Convert the warm audience. Activate the complimentary design consultation (framed as scarcity, not discount), launch the referral game, and lean into client-reveal moments. Goal: booked consultations and a referral loop that compounds past day 90.

Why multiple posts a day works here — without burning you out

You're not creating multiple shoots a day — you're atomizing one shoot into many posts. One jobsite visit with your camera + drone yields a drone reveal Reel, a process Reel, a carousel of material close-ups, 5–8 Story frames, and a TikTok recut. Batch-film one day a week on-site; the calendar below spaces that footage across 7 days. The "multiple daily" volume is a scheduling outcome, not a daily-filming burden. Reality check: until Project 1 is underway, days lean on Mike's LA photos, the Field Lead's past work, AI concept visualizations (always labeled), and talking-head/education content you can shoot anywhere.
