The Model Y wrap

Gio's round-one notes, applied to a copy of version 1: the site plan is now drawn in gold and fades in and out across the back half of the car, the passenger side reads logo first, the white band on the back is gone, and the phone number is twice the size.

What changed from version 1

  • Gold site plan. The pen-and-ink lines are SDLR gold, not one shade lighter than the green.
  • Fades in and out. The plan fades in across the rear door, runs full on the rear quarter, and fades out toward the tail light. On the liftgate it rises above the phone number.
  • Passenger side, logo first. SDLR lockup on the left of OUTDOOR LIVING / DESIGN + BUILD, same as the driver side.
  • No white band on the back. Phone and website sit in gold straight on the green.
  • Bigger phone number. 2.1 in caps on the doors (was 1.1 in) on its own line, website on its own line under it; 2.0 in on the liftgate.

The flat lay

Every panel unfolded at true scale (1:10): front bumper on top, hood and glass roof in the middle, driver side folded out left, passenger side right, liftgate and rear bumper at the bottom. This is the layout the wrap shop prints from.
