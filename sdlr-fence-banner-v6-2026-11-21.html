San Diego Landscape Remodeling · Fence banner · Version 6

The same banner, in SDLR’s own type

Versions 1–5 were set in Montserrat, copying the yard sign. SDLR’s brand guide (v1.2) says Montserrat lives only inside the logo, headlines are a serif in sentence case, everything else is Inter, and the call to action reads “Book a Consultation.” Version 6 keeps version 5’s order and layout and follows those rules.

What changed from version 5

  • Headline in Cormorant Garamond, the brand’s serif, in sentence case: Backyard in progress. Capitals 4.2 in tall, lowercase 2.6 in.
  • Everything else in Inter, the brand’s sans. Labels are uppercase and widely spaced, as in the guide’s eyebrows.
  • “Book a Consultation” above the phone, replacing “Call or text” (the brand guide’s call to action).
  • A short gold rule between the logo and the headline: the guide’s signature eyebrow-and-hairline move.
  • Same order as version 5: the headline first, the phone second, the logo third, details last. Same photo, same colors.

Print spec

Finished size96 × 48 in (8 × 4 ft). A 96 × 60 in (8 × 5 ft) file is also ready in Drive.

MaterialMesh vinyl (wind on a fence), hemmed edges, grommets every 2 ft, zip-tied to the fence.
ColorsSage-800 #2E382B, sage #4E5D4A, sage-700 #3E4A3A, gold #BDA572, paper #F5F1E8. No Pantone numbers yet: ask for a printed proof and check the greens do not go grey or the gold brassy.
