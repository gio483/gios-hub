House Car

Back to the quiet of version 10, with one idea: dress the car like the house car a grand hotel sends to the airport. The paint is the same midnight green metallic flake. Each front door carries only the SDLR crest, the stacked logo in gold, with the licence number small beneath it. Nothing else on the sides: no lettering stack, no badge. The phone and website move to the back, where people actually read them, sitting behind you at a red light. The car in these images is Gio's own 2025 Juniper Model Y.

On the street

Photo-style images made with Magnific (AI, Nano Banana Pro). They are concept images, not photos of the wrapped car. The side and rear images were made from Gio's own photos of his car. The side image's crest came out letter-perfect and is untouched. On the rear, the plate was painted blank and a reflection of the photographer in the bumper was painted out. Every word was proofread letter by letter.

The idea

  • The paint is the design; the crest signs it. The way a grand hotel dresses its house cars: one colour and one mark.
  • Even quieter than version 10. One gold mark per side instead of a seven-line stack on the door.
  • The stacked logo is the brand's primary mark ("where there is room, the stacked lockup is the brand"), and it already says what we do: San Diego Landscape Remodeling.
  • The number lives on the back. A side is seen for two seconds in passing; the back is read for a whole red light.
  • The simplest build of all: one colour-change film and a little cut vinyl.

Before it prints

  • Fix the licence name: CSLB lists "San Diego Landscaping Remodeling"; the car says "Landscape". Mike corrects the record first.
  • Film samples on the car in daylight, in shade and in sun: TeckWrap HM07 Green Black next to Inozetek MSG023 Metallic Midnight Green. Hold them next to the brand print green, Forest #2E382B (brand guide v1.4); the body is a colour-change film, so it is picked by eye.
  • Metallic gold vinyl samples next to the brand gold (#BDA572).
  • Quote the door jambs: the car is grey underneath, and grey shows against near-black green.
  • Measure every panel before cutting.
