How to post (60-second guide)
  1. Download the image — tap the gold Download button. On iPhone the share sheet pops up → tap Save Image to send it to Photos. (Desktop downloads the file directly.)
  2. Copy the caption — tap Copy caption + tags. It's now on your clipboard.
  3. Open Instagram → new post → pick the saved photo → paste the caption.
  4. Tap … / Advanced settings → Schedule and set the day & time shown on the card.
  5. Come back here and tap Mark scheduled ✓ so we know it's done.
Do all 5 in one sitting and the whole week is scheduled. New posts appear here automatically every Sunday evening.
