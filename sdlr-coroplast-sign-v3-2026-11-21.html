Five fixes: a reason to call, an easier QR, a bigger name

Gio asked whether this was my best work. I listed five things I'd improve as the marketing person, and he said "Do all five."

Flat, both sides

Side 1 on the left, side 2 on the right. They print back to back on one board.

Mockups: each side on a wire H-stake on a San Diego corner.

What changed

  • A reason to call: "See your yard before we build it" under BACKYARD REMODELS.
  • An easier QR: it opens the 60-second cost estimator, captioned "What would yours cost? Scan · no email needed".
  • A sign phone number: priced in GoHighLevel and approved for later. The phone is one live text frame, so the swap is quick.
  • A bigger logo: 15 in on side 1, so SAN DIEGO LANDSCAPE REMODELING reads from further away.
  • Brand craft: the Model Y wrap's pen-and-ink site plan, tone on tone, in every green field.
