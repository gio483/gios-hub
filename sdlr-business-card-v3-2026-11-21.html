What changed

  • The drawing shows the garden. The paving pattern (deck tiles, terrace brick) is left out, so the deck reads as calm open space. A wider crop brings in the planting beds, trees, lawn and jacaranda on every side.
  • Foil-ready. A separate foil set is ready: the colour artwork with the printed logo removed, plus a black foil mask. Every line in the logo is at least 0.5 pt, the minimum for hot-foil stampers such as Jukebox and AlphaGraphics. MOO's foil needs 1 pt lines, which this logo can't reach at business-card size, so order foil elsewhere.
  • A calmer back. "Book a Consultation" now sits on the bottom line, right beside the QR it belongs to. The email line ends above the QR, so Mike's long address no longer crowds it.
