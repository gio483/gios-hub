Two type voices On purpose

Street pieces use bold Montserrat capitals so they read from a car. Hand-held pieces use the Cormorant serif so they feel premium up close. Brand guide v1.3 allows both, and each family stays consistent inside itself.

Wording Repeats well

The same lines carry from piece to piece: "Backyard in progress · Ready to remodel yours?" on the banner and the coroplast sign, "Ready to remodel your backyard?" on the door hanger and the October sign, "By design, not by dispatch." on both postcards and the door hanger, and "Book a consultation" on the card.

Logo Approved forms only

The horizontal lockup is on the banner, the coroplast sign, the card and the back of the car, where height is tight. The stacked lockup is on the door hanger, the October sign, the postcards, the shirts and the car doors. Both are approved forms. The black tees also wrap the stacked logo in a round badge (San Diego · California / Outdoor Living). It's a shirt-only device: fine for merch, but it isn't in the brand guide.
