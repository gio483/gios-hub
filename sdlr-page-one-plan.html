Don't waste money on

  • "Guaranteed page 1." Nobody controls Google. What they sell is ranking for terms nobody searches.
  • Bought links. Fastest route to a penalty. With one real link today, a sudden thousand is unmissable.
  • Houzz and Yelp for link value. Fine for referrals and citations, but both block the link from passing ranking value.
  • More blog posts as the main lever. You have 114. Another hundred won't fix one backlink.
