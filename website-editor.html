Website Editor

Change the words on any of your websites yourself — no code. The editor runs on your Mac, where your site files live.

Checking if the editor is running…
Open the editor →
1

Start the editor on your Mac

In your Claude AI folder, open tools/site-editor and double-click Open Site Editor.command. A small black window opens — leave it running.

2

Click "Open the editor" above

It opens the editor in your browser. Pick a website at the top.

3

Edit and save

Click into any text, change the words like a Google Doc, then hit Save changes. Every save keeps a backup automatically.

Why a button instead of editing right here? Your website files live on your Mac, not on this hub. The editor has to run where the files are, so it runs locally. On your phone this page is just a reminder — the editing itself happens on your Mac.
