    The game plan

    1. Mail the core corridor first. La Mesa, Mt Helix, Del Cerro and San Carlos hold most of the pipeline and sit minutes from the office.
    2. Feed the website, it is the engine. Most leads and the 300k job came through it. Point ads and mailers at driving traffic to the site.
    3. Fix follow up. A 200k Vista job went cold from silence. Put every new lead on automatic follow up so big ones stop slipping.
    4. Coastal is a referral play. A 600k La Jolla and a 125k Solana Beach job both slipped. The coast has money but nothing closes cold. Work it through partners and designers.
    5. Close what you have. Thirteen open leads worth about 895k. Converting these beats chasing more.
